The one-line brief

Confident, technical, plain-spoken. A builder's brand, not an agency's.

Deep navy ground, gold for the one thing that matters, blue for structure.
Numbers over adjectives. Every claim is costed.
No stock photos, no gradients-as-decoration, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
01 · Logo

The mark is a hub, not a sun.

Eight nodes on spokes around a gold core: work routed out, results routed back. Flat, geometric, no shadow, no bevel. It reads at 24px, which is the only test that matters.

Primary mark
Use everywhere. SVG for screen, the 512px PNG where SVG isn't allowed. Never re-colour it.
Horizontal lockup · new
LFSA
DIGITAL
For wide spaces: email signatures, invoice headers, deck footers, video end-cards. Wordmark is Space Grotesk 700 / 600.
Clear space & sizes
Clear space = 25% of the mark's diameter on every side. Sizes in use: 44px nav, 30px footer, 24px minimum.
Do
  • Place on #0A2463, #061640, #F5C518 or white.
  • Keep the flat SVG — it is the master.
  • Use the lockup when the name isn't otherwise on screen.
Don't
  • Add drop shadows, bevels or glow.
  • Rotate, stretch, or crop the circle.
  • Sit it on a photo or a busy gradient.
Retire

Delete logo.png. It's a rendered 3D version of the mark with a dirty grey halo and a soft-shadow wordmark — it doesn't match the flat SVG and it can't sit on navy. The lockup above replaces it.

03 · Typography

Space Grotesk speaks. Inter explains.

Grotesk for anything structural — headings, buttons, labels, numbers, nav. Inter for anything you actually read in sentences. Never the reverse.

Headings, buttons, eyebrows, tags, step numbers, prices, stat values, nav links. Tight tracking on display sizes (−1.5px at 3.7rem), wide tracking on small caps labels (+3px).

Inter 400 · 500 · 600 · 700

Body copy, ledes, list items, captions, form input. Line-height 1.65 at body, 1.7 for long-form. Bold to 600 for emphasis inside a paragraph, never 700.

Section anatomy

Every section on the site is the same four moves. Sections alternate navy / deep-navy down the page, and the gold band is used exactly once, for the guarantee.

Eyebrow

A 24px blue rule then 3px-tracked caps. Centred variant gets a rule on both sides.

Heading.

Six words or fewer. Ends in a full stop. Gold on at most one phrase.

A lede that sets up what follows, in one or two sentences.

Muted, 1.1rem, capped at 600px. Then 44–52px of air before the content.

Rhythm

Section padding clamp(64px,8vw,104px), gutters clamp(20px,5vw,24px), content capped at 1100px.

Grid rule

Use auto-fit for card grids where the count can vary. For a fixed odd count — the five steps — declare explicit column counts per breakpoint, or the last item orphans onto its own row.

08 · Voice

Write like you're already building.

Short sentences. Full stops.

Headlines end in a period, not an exclamation mark. "Five steps. About a week." beats "Our proven 5-step methodology!"

Every claim is costed.

Hours, dollars, days. If a sentence has an adjective where a number could go, replace it.

Name the risk, then remove it.

"The only thing you risk is 45 minutes." Say what the buyer is afraid of before they do.

Second person, plain words.

"Your week", not "the client's operational cadence". No leverage, no synergy, no unlock, no journey.

Buttons name the thing.

"Read a sample report", not "Learn more". Price goes in the button when there is one.

Italicise the product name.

AI in Your Business — the "in Your Business" part is always italic, in body and in buttons.
