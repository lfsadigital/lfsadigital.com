Admin Hire

Find the missing files before sending a follow-up.

What came in
Client emails and accounting documents, with a list of required files and reporting dates.
What we built
A Slack-operated assistant checked dates and coverage against the checklist and updated a reviewable tracker.
What came out
The assistant prepared one local draft for review. It withdrew the draft after receiving complete files. No client message was sent.

AInvictus AI Receptionist

Send a call to the right schedule.

What came in
An inbound call and the business’s scheduling rules.
What we built
The receptionist qualified the inquiry and sent the request through the configured Jobber scheduling path.
What came out
The phone test produced a Jobber request, a dashboard record and internal notifications. The workflow also supports transfer to a person when needed.

Medical clinic intake

Keep social inquiries in one queue.

What came in
Incoming Instagram and Facebook messages, sometimes split across several messages in one conversation.
What we built
The assistant grouped related messages, kept the conversation context, checked approved knowledge and updated the lead queue.
What came out
Urgent, clinical or uncertain questions went to a person. The public evidence contains sanitized workflow diagrams and no patient records.
